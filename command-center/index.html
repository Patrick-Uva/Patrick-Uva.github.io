Command Center

A private, single-user personal planning app that runs on its owner's own computer.

Command Center helps one person plan their week: tasks, school assignments, habits, notes and a weekly planner. It is not a public service — there are no accounts to sign up for, and it is not offered to other users.

Google Calendar

With the owner's permission, Command Center reads their Google Calendar (read-only) so that their existing appointments appear in the weekly planner and clashes with planned study time can be spotted. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar.

  • Permissions requested: view events, and see the list of calendars.
  • Data stays on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
